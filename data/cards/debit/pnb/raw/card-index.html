Rupay National Common Mobility Card (NCMC)

The RuPay National Common Mobility Card (NCMC) is a contactless payment solution designed to facilitate seamless transactions across transport, retail, and parking. Launched with the vision of “One Nation One Card,” this card aims to integrate multiple payment needs into a single card, promoting convenience and efficiency for users. RuPay NCMC is part of India`s initiative to create a unified and interoperable payment system. Key Features:

  • Contactless Payments: Make quick and secure transactions like never before by simply tapping the card on contactless-enabled terminals.
  • Interoperability: Your RuPay NCMC Debit Card can be used across different modes of transit like metros and buses. Hence, no need to carry separate cards to travel in different metros and buses. Check out the list of transit projects live with NCMC by clicking the following link. List of Live Projects. Click for list of operators running NCMC - Projects live on NCMC
  • Offline Transactions: No network? No problem. Your RuPay NCMC Debit Card also works offline for transit.
  • Multi-Purpose: One card for all your needs. Shop and travel with just one RuPay NCMC Debit Card.
  • Enhanced Security: Enjoy stress-free transactions with the trustworthiness of RuPay Network. It is safe and secure.
